# Sound effect sources

The converted `.ogg` files under `audio/` come from the user-provided sources below.

- `music/omusubi_bouken.ogg` — user-provided `8bit-omusubi-bouken.mp3`

- `footstep_grass.ogg` — FreeSFX, `GameSFX/FootStep/Retro FootStep Grass 01.wav`
- `ball_impact.ogg` — Shapeforms Audio Free Sound Effects, `Hit and Punch Preview/AUDIO/HIT_SHORT_04.wav`
- `ball_burst.ogg` — FreeSFX, `GameSFX/Explosion/Retro Explosion Short 01.wav`
- `gate_blocked.ogg` — Shapeforms Audio Free Sound Effects, `Hit and Punch Preview/AUDIO/PUNCH_PERCUSSIVE_HEAVY_08.wav`
- `gate_break.ogg` — FreeSFX, `GameSFX/Explosion/Retro Explosion Short 15.wav`
- `level_clear.ogg` — FreeSFX, `GameSFX/Music/Success/Retro Success Melody 04 - electric piano 2.wav`
- `ui_click.ogg` — Shapeforms Audio Free Sound Effects, `Type Preview/Tablet/Tablet_Tap_07.wav`
- `ui_ready.ogg` — FreeSFX, `GameSFX/Events/Retro Event UI StereoUP 01.wav`
- `ui_error.ogg` — FreeSFX, `GameSFX/Events/Wrong/Retro Event Wrong Simple 03.wav`

All files were converted to Ogg Vorbis without changing their timing. The UI click was gain-adjusted for audibility.
